Fifteen of the best sky events from 1999 to 2027. Pick a view and Solar Voyager takes you to that exact moment, with every world where it really was.

    Times are the moment of greatest eclipse or closest approach. Planet positions come from JPL orbital elements and the Moon’s from the ELP-2000/82 lunar theory, so each eclipse happens within about a minute of the published time.
