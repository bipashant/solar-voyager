Sizes and distances are to true scale, so most worlds are tiny dots until you get close. Planet positions follow the real date: Earth's day side and the Moon's phase match the sky right now. Cinematic flights compress time, and the ship clock shows how long the trip really takes at the speed you chose.

Planet maps: Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), based on NASA imagery. Orbits: JPL approximate Keplerian elements. Moons without spacecraft maps are generated from their known colours and terrain.
